================================================================
HOW TO MAKE INSANE-REALISTIC AI VIDEOS — THE FORMULA
(AAAI Event Hub @ TCET | use with Firefly Generate Video,
Runway Gen-3, Kling, or Luma — same structure works everywhere)
================================================================

THE 9-LAYER FORMULA — build every prompt in this exact order:

[1] SHOT & CAMERA   -> what kind of shot + how the camera moves
[2] LENS & FORMAT   -> focal length, aperture, film look
[3] SUBJECT         -> who/what, with precise physical detail
[4] ACTION          -> what happens, in what order
[5] ENVIRONMENT     -> where, with specific textures/materials
[6] LIGHTING        -> every light source, direction and colour
[7] MOTION PHYSICS  -> speed, weight, slow-motion feel, loop point
[8] ATMOSPHERE      -> haze, dust, bokeh, reflections
[9] GRADE & QUALITY -> colour palette, camera body, resolution tags

WHY VIDEOS LOOK FAKE WITHOUT THESE:
- No [2]: plastic "AI sheen"        - No [7]: floaty, weightless motion
- No [6]: flat grey lighting        - No [8]: sterile clean air
- No [9]: cartoonish saturation     - No loop note: jarring cuts

UNIVERSAL QUALITY BLOCK (paste at end of every video prompt):
"shot on ARRI Alexa 65 with Panavision Sphero 65 lenses, T2.0,
8K resolution downsampled to 4K, 24fps natural motion blur,
photorealistic, physically accurate light falloff, subtle sensor
grain, HDR colour depth, no text, no watermark, no logos"

TCET COLOUR GRADE BLOCK (the site's identity):
"colour grade: crushed inky blacks (#000-#1d1d1f), warm tungsten
highlights, electric blue #2E6FF2 and violet #7B5CFA accent
lights, gentle film halation around bright edges"

LOOP RULE: describe frame 1 == last frame.
e.g. "first and final frames identical for a seamless loop"

MOTION RULES (this is 80% of realism):
- one hero motion per clip; everything else drifts slowly
- name the speed: "slow dolly at walking pace", "120fps slow motion"
- give objects weight: "fabric settles heavily", "badge sways on its lanyard"
- people: small gestures only (clapping, typing, walking away from cam)
- never let faces turn toward camera (avoids AI face artifacts)

NEGATIVE PROMPT (every tool):
"gibberish text, warped faces, morphing hands, extra fingers,
flicker, jitter, cartoon, CGI plastic look, oversaturated,
low resolution, watermark, logo, jump cut"

RECOMMENDED SETTINGS PER CLIP:
- duration 4-5s (loops) / 8s (hero)   - aspect 16:9
- generate 3-4 takes per prompt, pick the one with steadiest motion
- upscale + convert to webm/mov sizes AFTER picking the winner
